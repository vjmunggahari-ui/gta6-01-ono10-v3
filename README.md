An open-city crime story set in Solmar Bay. Jax Marlowe owes the crew: work the jobs, dodge the Solmar PD, and own the bay — on foot or behind the wheel.

## Play

Open the game in a desktop browser from static files, no network needed:

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080

WASD to walk and drive, E to steal or exit a car, F to punch, M for the next job, Esc to pause, H to mute. Progress saves on your machine.

## The city

A 3200×2400 open world: downtown streets, a beach, a marina, traffic, pedestrians, and a five-star wanted system with police chases. Three crew jobs — First Fare, Hot Package, Bay Getaway — plus street fares between jobs.
